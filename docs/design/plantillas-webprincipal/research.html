Journal Article 2024

Information Asymmetry in Emerging Carbon Markets: A Mechanism Design Approach

with J. Doe and E. Smith

The Quarterly Journal of Economics, Vol. 139 (2)

picture_as_pdf PDF link DOI
This paper explores the efficiency of emerging carbon trading systems under significant information asymmetry regarding abatement costs. We propose a novel mechanism that achieves second-best efficiency by leveraging sequential auditing and non-linear pricing. Our empirical application to the EU ETS demonstrates substantial welfare gains compared to uniform price auctions.
Working Paper 2023

The Political Economy of Incentive Design in Bureaucracies

Solo Authored

NBER Working Paper Series, No. 31456

picture_as_pdf PDF
We analyze how political oversight influences the design of incentive contracts within government agencies. When politicians are risk-averse regarding bureaucratic failures, optimal contracts heavily distort incentives away from high-variance innovative tasks towards routine administration.
